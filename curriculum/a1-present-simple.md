## Rule
We use the present simple for routines, habits and facts.
With **I, you, we, they** the verb doesn't change: I **work**, they **play**.
Negative: **don't** + verb → I **don't** like coffee.
Frequency words come **before** the main verb: always, usually, often, sometimes, never.
With *to be* they come **after**: I **am always** tired.
Em português: o presente simples é para coisas que acontecem sempre ou normalmente, não para o que está acontecendo agora.

## Examples
- Kids: I **play** with my friends after school. I **never** eat broccoli!
- Teens: We **usually** watch series on Friday.
- Adults: I **work** from home on Mondays. I **don't drink** coffee at night.
- Time words: every day, on weekends, in the morning.

## Common mistakes
- "I no like" → "I don't like". A negação usa **don't**.
- "I always am late" → "I am always late". Com *to be*, a frequência vem depois.
- "I go always" → "I always go". Com outros verbos, vem antes.
- "I am work every day" → "I work every day". Não misture *to be* com outro verbo.
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".

## Practice
1. I ___ (study) English every day. → study
2. They ___ (not / eat) meat. → don't eat
3. We / sometimes / play / soccer → We sometimes play soccer.
4. I / late / never / am → I am never late.
