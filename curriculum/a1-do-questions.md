## Rule
To ask questions in the present simple, put **do** or **does** before the subject:
- **Do** + I / you / we / they + verb → **Do** you like pizza?
- **Does** + he / she / it + verb (no -s) → **Does** she speak English?
Question words come first: **Where do** you live? **What does** he do?
Short answers: Yes, I **do**. / No, she **doesn't**.
The verb *to be* doesn't use do: **Are** you tired? (not "Do you are tired?").
Em português, a pergunta é a frase afirmativa com entonação; em inglês, precisa do *do/does* no começo.

## Examples
- Kids: **Do** you have a pet? — Yes, I **do**!
- Teens: **Does** your brother play video games?
- Adults: **Where do** you work? **What time does** the store open?

## Common mistakes
- "You like coffee?" → "Do you like coffee?" A pergunta precisa de **do**.
- "Does she likes?" → "Does she like?" Com *does*, o verbo fica sem -s.
- "Where you live?" → "Where do you live?"
- "Do you are happy?" → "Are you happy?" Com *to be*, não use *do*.
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".

## Practice
1. ___ you speak Spanish? → Do
2. ___ he work on Saturdays? → Does
3. Where ___ they live? → do
4. Does she ___ (watch) TV? → watch
