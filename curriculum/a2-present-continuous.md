## Rule
We use the present continuous for actions happening **now** or around now, and for fixed future plans.
Form: **am / is / are + verb-ing** → I **am reading**, she **is working**, they **are playing**.
Spelling: drop the final -e (make → making); double one final consonant after a short vowel (run → running, sit → sitting).
Negative: I'm **not** working. Question: **Are** you **listening**?
Some verbs are not normally used in the continuous: like, love, want, know, need, understand.
Em português: equivale ao "estou fazendo". Para rotinas, use o presente simples, não o contínuo.

## Examples
- Kids: Look! The cat **is sleeping** on my bed.
- Teens: I **am listening** to my favorite song right now.
- Adults: Sorry, I **am driving**. I'll call you back. We **are meeting** the client tomorrow.
- Now vs. routine: I **work** in an office, but today I **am working** from home.

## Common mistakes
- "I working now" → "I am working now". Não esqueça o *am / is / are*.
- "She is play" → "She is playing". O verbo precisa do -ing.
- "I am wanting a coffee" → "I want a coffee". *Want* não costuma ir para o -ing.
- "I am going to the gym every day" (routine) → "I go to the gym every day".
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".

## Practice
1. Be quiet! The baby ___ (sleep). → is sleeping
2. They ___ (watch) a movie now. → are watching
3. I ___ (not / work) today. → am not working
4. ___ you ___ (listen) to me? → Are, listening
