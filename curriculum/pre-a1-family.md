## Rule
Family words: mother (mom), father (dad), parents, brother, sister, son, daughter, grandmother (grandma),
grandfather (grandpa), uncle, aunt, cousin, husband, wife.
Possessives: **my** (meu/minha), **your** (seu/sua), **his** (dele), **her** (dela).
To say something belongs to someone, use **'s**: **Ana's** brother = o irmão da Ana.
Em português: "his" e "her" dependem de quem possui, não da coisa. "Her brother" = o irmão dela.

## Examples
- Kids: This is my **mom**. This is my **grandpa**. I love my family!
- Teens: My **sister** is 15. Her name is Julia.
- Adults: My **wife** is a teacher. Our **son** is five years old.
- Possessive 's: This is **Pedro's** car.

## Common mistakes
- "the brother of Ana" → "Ana's brother". Em inglês usamos 's para posse.
- "She is my brother" → "She is my sister". Brother é homem, sister é mulher.
- "His mother" when talking about a woman's mother → "Her mother". Use *her* quando a dona é uma mulher.
- "My parents" means pai e mãe, not "parentes" (relatives = parentes).
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".

## Practice
1. My mother's mother is my ___. → grandmother
2. Maria has a son. ___ son is ten. → Her
3. The car of Paulo → ___ car → Paulo's
4. My father's brother is my ___. → uncle
