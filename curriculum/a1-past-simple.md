## Rule
We use the past simple for finished actions in the past.
Regular verbs take **-ed**: work → work**ed**, play → play**ed**, study → stud**ied**.
Some verbs change completely in the past (irregular): go → **went**, have → **had**, see → **saw**,
eat → **ate**, buy → **bought**, make → **made**.
The past is the same for all people: I went, she went, they went.
Negative and questions use **didn't / did** + base verb: I **didn't go**. **Did** you **see** it?
Em português: alguns verbos não ganham -ed no passado; eles mudam de forma.

## Examples
- Kids: Yesterday I **went** to the park. I **saw** a big dog!
- Teens: I **watched** a great video last night.
- Adults: I **had** a meeting yesterday. We **went** to Lisbon last year.
- Question: **Did** you **buy** milk? — No, I **didn't**.

## Common mistakes
- "Yesterday I go" → "Yesterday I went". Words like *yesterday*, *last week*, *ago* pedem passado.
- "I goed" → "I went". *Go* é irregular.
- "I didn't went" → "I didn't go". Depois de *didn't*, o verbo volta ao normal.
- "Did you saw?" → "Did you see?"
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".

## Practice
1. Yesterday I ___ (go) to school. → went
2. She ___ (see) a cat. → saw
3. We ___ (play) soccer last Sunday. → played
4. I didn't ___ (eat) breakfast. → eat
