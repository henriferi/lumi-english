## Rule
We greet people with **Hello** or **Hi** (informal). The time of day changes the greeting:
**Good morning** (until noon), **Good afternoon** (after noon), **Good evening** (at night, when you arrive).
**Good night** is only for saying goodbye or going to bed.
To introduce yourself: **My name is ...** or **I'm ...**. To ask a name: **What's your name?**
To answer "How are you?": **I'm fine, thanks. And you?**
Em português: "Good night" não é o "boa noite" de quando você chega, só o de despedida ou antes de dormir. Ao chegar à noite, diga "Good evening".

## Examples
- Kids: Hi! My name is Leo. What's your name?
- Teens: Hey! I'm Bia. Nice to meet you!
- Adults: Good morning. My name is Carla. Nice to meet you.
- Answering: How are you? — I'm fine, thanks. And you?

## Common mistakes
- "My name are Ana" → "My name is Ana". Com *my name* usamos **is**.
- "I have 20 years" → "I'm 20 (years old)". Em inglês, idade usa o verbo *to be*, não *have*.
- Saying "Good night" when arriving → "Good evening".
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".
