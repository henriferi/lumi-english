## Rule
Numbers 1 to 10: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten.
11 to 20: eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty.
Tens: thirty, forty, fifty, sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety, one hundred.
Colors: red, blue, yellow, green, black, white, orange, pink, purple, brown, gray.
In English the color comes **before** the noun: a **red** car, a **blue** shirt. Colors never take -s: two red cars.
Em português: a cor vem depois do nome ("carro vermelho"); em inglês ela vem antes ("red car") e não vai para o plural.

## Examples
- Kids: I have **three** cats. One cat is **black**.
- Teens: My phone is **white** and my headphones are **pink**.
- Adults: The meeting is in room **fourteen**. I drive a **gray** car.
- Asking: How many? — **Two**. / What color is it? — It's **green**.

## Common mistakes
- "a car red" → "a red car". A cor vem antes do nome.
- "two reds cars" → "two red cars". Adjetivos não têm plural em inglês.
- "fourty" → "forty". O número 40 não tem "u".
- Mixing up "thirteen" (13) and "thirty" (30): in -teen the stress is at the end, in -ty at the beginning.
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".
