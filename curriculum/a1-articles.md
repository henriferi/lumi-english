## Rule
**a / an** = um, uma (one thing, not specific). Use **a** before a consonant **sound** and **an** before a vowel **sound**:
a book, a car, a university (sounds like "you"); an apple, an egg, an hour (the h is silent).
**the** = o, a, os, as (a specific thing, or something we already know): **the** sun, **the** book on the table.
Jobs need a / an: She is **a** doctor. He is **an** engineer.
Em português: dizemos "ela é médica" sem artigo, mas em inglês a profissão precisa de *a* ou *an*.

## Examples
- Kids: I have **a** cat and **an** orange. **The** cat is sleeping.
- Teens: I want **a** new phone. **The** phone in this video is amazing.
- Adults: I'm **an** accountant. Can you close **the** door, please?
- Plural with no article: I like dogs. (in general)

## Common mistakes
- "a apple" → "an apple". Antes de som de vogal, use **an**.
- "an university" → "a university". O som inicial é "iu", de consoante.
- "She is teacher" → "She is a teacher". Profissão precisa de artigo.
- "I like the dogs" (in general) → "I like dogs". Para falar em geral, sem artigo.
- How to correct: praise the attempt, show the right form, ask the learner to try again. Never say "wrong".

## Practice
1. I eat ___ egg every day. → an
2. He is ___ pilot. → a
3. Please open ___ window. → the
4. She has ___ hour for lunch. → an
